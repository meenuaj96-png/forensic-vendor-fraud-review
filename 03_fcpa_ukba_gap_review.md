# Anti-Bribery Compliance Gap Review (FICTIONAL)

**Assumption:** Meridian is the Indian subsidiary of a group with a US listing and UK business operations, so FCPA and UK Bribery Act exposure is plausible. Indian law applies regardless.

## Legal frameworks (summary, for learning; verify against current official text)
| Law | Relevance here |
|---|---|
| **US FCPA** | Anti-bribery provisions (paying anything of value to a foreign official to obtain or retain business or an improper advantage) plus accounting provisions (accurate books and records, internal controls). Hospitality for customs officials and mislabelled "facilitation" payments raise both. |
| **UK Bribery Act 2010** | Offences of bribing, being bribed, bribing a foreign public official (s.6) and corporate failure to prevent bribery (s.7), with an "adequate procedures" defence. It has **no exception for facilitation payments**. |
| **Prevention of Corruption Act 1988 (as amended 2018)** | Offences relating to public servants and a corporate offence for bribes given by commercial organisations, with an adequate-procedures defence. |
| **PMLA 2002** | Proceeds of crime and layering risk if payments are routed through offshore accounts. |
| **Companies Act 2013** | Fraud provisions (s.447) and auditor duty to report suspected fraud (s.143(12)). |

## Gap assessment against the UKBA six principles
| Principle | Observed position | Gap | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proportionate procedures | No written ABC policy covering agents or hospitality | Policy missing | High |
| Top-level commitment | No evidence of board tone or ABC statement | Unclear | Medium |
| Risk assessment | No third-party or country/sector risk assessment (customs and port interaction is high risk) | Missing | High |
| Due diligence | Vendors onboarded by one person, no KYC or beneficial ownership checks | Failed | High |
| Communication and training | No ABC training evidence | Missing | Medium |
| Monitoring and review | Threshold splitting and duplicates not detected for 12 months | Controls ineffective | High |

## FCPA internal controls and books-and-records points
- Payments described as "consulting" or "clearance" without deliverables may be **mis-described in the books**.
- Single-approver onboarding and payment approval is a segregation-of-duties failure.
- Hospitality for officials has no pre-approval or register.

## Remediation roadmap
| Priority | Action | Owner (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | Evidence preservation, payment hold, legal counsel engaged | Legal / Audit Committee |
| 30 days | ABC policy, gifts and hospitality policy, prohibition of facilitation payments | Compliance |
| 60 days | Third-party risk assessment and due diligence on all agents and intermediaries | Compliance / Procurement |
| 90 days | System controls: split-payment alerts, duplicate blocks, dual onboarding approval | Finance / IT |
| Ongoing | Training, hotline promotion, periodic audit and monitoring | Compliance / Internal Audit |
